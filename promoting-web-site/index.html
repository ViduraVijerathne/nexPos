FAQ

Questions teams usually ask before rollout.

Can NexPos work when internet is unstable?

Yes. NexPos supports an offline-first database and can also run in a Firebase-powered online model when your shop needs cloud visibility.

Is it only a billing screen?

No. It also covers products, stock, GRN, suppliers, customers, invoices, expenses, reports, backups, and subscription usage.

Can invoices and reports be customized?

Yes. NexPos includes configurable invoice layouts, report headers, paper sizes, fonts, margins, export, and print flows.
